02 — PERCEPTION & SITUATIONAL AWARENESS

See the whole picture — and prove it.

Sensors, imagery and open signals fused into one explorable, uncertainty-aware picture.

SA·01Flagship

DRISHTI

SA Monitor · CV + Geospatial

A multi-camera situational-awareness platform. It fuses many cameras into one global track picture, recovers PTZ pose from the video itself, and places every target on real terrain with ± uncertainty — then predicts motion along the road network.

Fusion: ≈6–14 mEdge: ~50 fps
SA·02Field-trialed

GeoVision

GEOINT · Media Geolocation

Geolocates a photo or video from its own content through a five-stage pipeline — vision, AI clue extraction, geocoding, a Street-View & satellite verification loop, and an independent cross-check — with an honest uncertainty radius.

Pipeline: 5-stage verifyOutput: pin ± radius

OSINT Recon

Intelligence · Correlation Graph

Give it a seed — a domain, email, username, phone or IP — and it fans out across connectors, auto-pivots, and collapses everything into one deduped entity graph. Authorized-use gated.

Model: entity graphMethod: auto-pivot
